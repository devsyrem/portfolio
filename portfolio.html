01 / About

I work at the intersection of blockchain security and systems engineering — designing infrastructure that keeps private keys off the network entirely. At Coldstar, I co-built an open-source cold-signing system that turns a standard USB drive into a disposable, RAM-only signing medium for Solana: keys are decrypted only in locked memory for the duration of signing, then wiped, with no permanent trusted hardware or secure element required.

I also led development of RugGuard, a browser extension that acts as a token-level scam detector across major crypto platforms, and I've spent time on the forensic and analytical side with a Solana on-chain forensic toolkit. My background bridges cyber security, digital forensics, and aerospace engineering — I think about systems in terms of failure modes and attack surface first.

Outside of engineering, I tutor GCSE Computer Science students and mentor non-technical founders on shipping and securing their products.
